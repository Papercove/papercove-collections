# Cybersecurity

Curated by: Papercove  
Language: English  
Description: Security news, breach analysis and research from people who actually do the work. Signal over fear.

- [Krebs on Security](https://krebsonsecurity.com/feed/): Brian Krebs's investigative reporting on cybercrime, breaches and the people behind them, about weekly.
- [Datadog Security Labs](https://securitylabs.datadoghq.com/rss/feed.xml): In-depth research on cloud threats, attacker techniques in AWS and Kubernetes, and how to detect them, a few posts a month.
- [Scott Helme](https://scotthelme.co.uk/rss/): Practical web security, from HTTPS and certificates to security headers, backed by real-world measurements. A few posts a month.
- [Citizen Lab](https://citizenlab.ca/feed/): Rigorous research on spyware, surveillance and digital threats to civil society, a few reports a month.
- [DoublePulsar](https://doublepulsar.com/feed): Kevin Beaumont's frank analysis of major vulnerabilities and incidents, often ahead of the news, about once a month.
- [Trail of Bits](https://blog.trailofbits.com/index.xml): Security research, tooling and audit lessons from one of the most respected security firms, about weekly.
- [Kim Zetter](https://www.zetter-zeroday.com/rss/): Zero Day, independent reporting on cyberwarfare, nation-state hacking and election security, a couple of times a month.
- [PortSwigger Research](https://portswigger.net/research/rss): Cutting-edge web security research from the team behind Burp Suite, about once a month.
- [Matthew Green](https://blog.cryptographyengineering.com/feed/): A Johns Hopkins cryptographer explaining how real-world cryptography works and fails, every month or two.
- [watchTowr Labs](https://labs.watchtowr.com/rss/): Irreverent, highly technical breakdowns of vulnerabilities in enterprise software, a couple of posts a month.
- [Filippo Valsorda](https://words.filippo.io/rss/): Cryptography engineering and open source maintenance from the former Go security lead, about once a month.
- [The DFIR Report](https://thedfirreport.com/feed/): Detailed reconstructions of real intrusions, from initial access to ransomware, every couple of months.
- [Lesley Carhart](https://tisiphone.net/feed/): Industrial control system security and incident response, plus honest career advice, every couple of months.
- [Volexity](https://www.volexity.com/feed/): Threat intelligence and incident response findings on advanced attackers, several reports a year.
- [tl;dr sec](https://rss.beehiiv.com/feeds/xgTKUmMmUm.xml): Clint Gibler's weekly digest of the best security tools, research and talks, carefully curated.
- [Aikido Security](https://www.aikido.dev/blog/rss.xml): Fast, detailed research on supply chain attacks, compromised packages and critical vulnerabilities, alongside product content. Several posts a day.
- [This Week in Security](https://this.weekinsecurity.com/rss/): Zack Whittaker's weekly roundup of the security news that actually matters.
- [Latacora](https://www.latacora.com/blog/index.xml): Practical security engineering advice for startups, from cryptography to cloud, every couple of months.
- [Phil Venables](https://www.philvenables.com/blog-feed.xml): A veteran CISO on security strategy, risk and leadership, a couple of essays a month.
- [Detection Engineering Weekly](https://www.detectionengineering.net/feed): Zack Allen's weekly newsletter on threat detection, SOC work and the research defenders should read.
