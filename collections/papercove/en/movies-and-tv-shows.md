# Movies & TV shows

Curated by: Papercove  
Language: English  
Description: Film and TV reviews, essays and criticism for people who love what's on screen, from blockbusters and prestige series to arthouse.

- [Observations on Film Art](https://www.davidbordwell.net/blog/feed/): Kristin Thompson and the late David Bordwell's deeply informed blog on how films work, several posts a year.
- [Letterboxd Journal](https://letterboxd.com/journal/rss/): Interviews, lists and essays from the film-lovers' social network, almost daily.
- [The Sheila Variations](https://www.sheilaomalley.com/?feed=rss2): Critic Sheila O'Malley's personal, generous writing on film, actors and TV, plus gems from her archives. Several posts a week.
- [MUBI Notebook](https://mubi.com/notebook/posts.atom): Thoughtful criticism, festival coverage and interviews on world and arthouse cinema, a few posts a week.
- [Nathan Rabin's Happy Place](https://www.nathanrabin.com/happy-place?format=rss): A former A.V. Club head writer's funny, heartfelt pop culture columns on films old and new, about one a day.
- [Rolling Stone Reviews](https://www.rollingstone.com/tv-movies/tv-movie-reviews/feed/): TV and film reviews from Alan Sepinwall, David Fear and team, a few a week.
- [John August](https://johnaugust.com/feed): A screenwriter on the craft and business of writing for film and TV, a few posts a month.
- [The Hollywood Reporter TV Reviews](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/c/tv/tv-reviews/feed/): Reviews of the new series worth knowing about, almost daily.
- [The Hollywood Reporter Movie Reviews](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/c/movies/movie-reviews/feed/): Reviews of new releases, from studio films to festival premieres, almost daily.
- [Bright Wall/Dark Room](https://www.brightwalldarkroom.com/feed/): Personal, beautifully written essays about the movies that shape us, a few a month.
- [Slant TV](https://www.slantmagazine.com/category/tv/feed/): Sharp, opinionated TV criticism, a couple of posts a week.
- [NPR Pop Culture](https://feeds.npr.org/1048/rss.xml): Reviews and conversations about movies, TV and culture from NPR, a few posts a week.
- [Variety Film Reviews](https://variety.com/v/film/reviews/feed/): Reviews of new releases and festival films from Variety's critics, about one a day.
- [/Film Reviews](https://www.slashfilm.com/category/movie-reviews/feed/): Accessible reviews of the big releases and buzzy indies, about one a day.
- [Collider Reviews](https://collider.com/feed/category/movie-reviews/): Reviews of new movies, from blockbusters to streaming releases, about one a day.
- [BBC Culture](https://www.bbc.com/culture/feed.rss): Smart, accessible stories on film, TV, music and art from the BBC, about one a day.
- [Den of Geek Movies](https://www.denofgeek.com/movies/feed/): Genre films and franchises, from Marvel and Star Wars to horror, with reviews and features. About one or two a day.
