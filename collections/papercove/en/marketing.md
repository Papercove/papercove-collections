# Marketing

Curated by: Papercove  
Language: English  
Description: Marketing, SEO and copywriting that respects the reader. Practical ideas, no growth-hack noise.

- [Seth Godin](https://seths.blog/feed/): Short daily reflections on marketing, permission and doing work that matters.
- [SparkToro](https://sparktoro.com/blog/feed/): Rand Fishkin and Amanda Natividad on audience research, zero-click marketing and smarter distribution, a few posts a month.
- [Ann Handley](https://annhandley.com/feed/): Warm, practical advice on writing and content marketing from the author of Everybody Writes, a couple of times a month.
- [Moz](https://moz.com/feeds/blog.rss): Hands-on SEO tactics, case studies and industry analysis, one or two posts a week.
- [Growth Memo](https://www.growth-memo.com/feed): Kevin Indig's data-driven analysis of SEO, AI search and organic growth, about twice a week.
- [Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/static/search/blog/feed.xml): Official updates on Google Search, ranking and structured data, straight from the source. A few posts a month.
- [Aleyda Solis](https://www.aleydasolis.com/en/feed/): International and technical SEO, AI search and practical frameworks from a leading consultant, about twice a month.
- [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/feed/): Research-backed SEO and AI search studies, with large datasets and step-by-step guides. Several posts a week.
- [Marketoonist](https://marketoonist.com/feed): Tom Fishburne's weekly cartoon poking fun at marketing clichés, with a short essay behind each one.
- [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/rss/): Social media marketing, transparent experiments and lessons from running a small, open company. Several posts a week.
- [Wes Kao](https://newsletter.weskao.com/feed): Sharp lessons on persuasive writing, positioning and communicating clearly at work, a couple of times a month.
- [Baymard Institute](https://baymard.com/blog/feed.xml): Large-scale e-commerce UX research on checkout, product pages and conversion, a few posts a month.
- [MKT1](https://newsletter.mkt1.co/feed): Emily Kramer on building B2B startup marketing: teams, programs, positioning and launches. Every week or two.
- [Foundation](https://foundationinc.co/lab/feed/): B2B content strategy and distribution, with detailed breakdowns of what top brands do. A couple of posts a week.
- [Elena Verna](https://www.elenaverna.com/feed): Product-led growth, acquisition and retention from a seasoned growth leader, every week or two.
- [Grow and Convert](https://www.growandconvert.com/feed/): Content marketing judged on conversions, not traffic, with real client data. About twice a month.
- [Mark Schaefer](https://businessesgrow.com/feed/): Marketing strategy, brand building and the human side of marketing, about once a week.
- [Jon Loomer](https://www.jonloomer.com/feed/): Hands-on, no-fluff guidance on Meta advertising, from targeting and testing to new ad features, a couple of posts a week.
- [Occam's Razor](https://www.kaushik.net/avinash/feed/): Avinash Kaushik's deep, opinionated essays on marketing analytics and measurement, every couple of months.
- [Backlinko](https://backlinko.com/feed): In-depth, well-illustrated SEO guides and studies, refreshed for AI search. A couple of posts a month.
