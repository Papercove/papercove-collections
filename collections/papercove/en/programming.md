# Programming

Curated by: Papercove  
Language: English  
Description: Engineers who write thoughtfully about code, systems and the craft of software.

- [Julia Evans](https://jvns.ca/atom.xml): Friendly, curious deep dives into Linux, networking and the tools we use every day, about once a month.
- [GitHub Engineering](https://github.blog/engineering/feed/): How GitHub builds and runs GitHub: architecture, infrastructure and developer tooling, a few posts a month.
- [Dan Luu](https://danluu.com/atom.xml): Long, data-heavy essays on software, hardware and how engineering organizations really work. Rare but always worth it.
- [Dropbox Tech](https://dropbox.tech/feed): In-depth engineering stories on storage, infrastructure and ML at Dropbox, about twice a month.
- [matklad](https://matklad.github.io/feed.xml): Sharp notes on language design, testing and systems programming from a Rust and Zig veteran, a few times a month.
- [Fly.io](https://fly.io/blog/feed.xml): Funny, opinionated posts on running a global cloud, from kernel internals to SQLite. About monthly.
- [Eli Bendersky](https://eli.thegreenplace.net/feeds/all.atom.xml): Clear, code-first explanations of compilers, Go, Python and machine learning internals, a few posts a month.
- [Oxide Computer](https://oxide.computer/blog/feed): Hardware and software built from scratch in Rust, told by the engineers doing it. About monthly.
- [Charity Majors](https://charity.wtf/feed): Blunt, insightful essays on observability, shipping to production and what makes engineers and teams good, a couple of posts a month.
- [TigerBeetle](https://tigerbeetle.com/blog/atom.xml): Database engineering, deterministic simulation testing and Zig, from the TigerBeetle team. About monthly.
- [Marc Brooker](https://brooker.co.za/blog/rss.xml): An AWS distinguished engineer on distributed systems, databases and reliability, explained with rigor, twice a month.
- [Shopify Engineering](https://shopify.engineering/blog.atom): Scaling Rails, Ruby internals and large-scale commerce infrastructure, a few posts a month.
- [null program](https://nullprogram.com/feed/): Chris Wellons on C, low-level tricks and minimal tooling, with lots of code. Roughly monthly.
- [Spotify Engineering](https://engineering.atspotify.com/feed): Backend, data, mobile and developer experience at Spotify, a few posts a month.
- [Vicki Boykis](https://vickiboykis.com/index.xml): Thoughtful writing on machine learning, data engineering and the craft of building software, about once a month.
- [Airbnb Engineering](https://medium.com/feed/airbnb-engineering): Architecture, data and frontend engineering stories from Airbnb, a few posts a month.
- [Computer Things](https://buttondown.com/hillelwayne/rss): Hillel Wayne's weekly newsletter on formal methods, software history and thinking clearly about code.
- [Pinterest Engineering](https://medium.com/feed/pinterest-engineering): Infrastructure, machine learning at scale and data engineering behind Pinterest, a few posts a month.
- [Zed](https://zed.dev/blog.rss): The team behind the Zed editor on Rust, GPU rendering, collaborative editing and editor architecture, mixed with product updates. About weekly.
- [The Go Blog](https://go.dev/blog/feed.atom): The Go team on language design, performance and tooling, carefully explained. About once a month.
