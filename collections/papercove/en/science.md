# Science

Curated by: Papercove  
Language: English  
Description: Accessible, well-researched science writing across physics, biology, space and beyond.

- [Quanta Magazine](https://www.quantamagazine.org/feed/): Award-winning explanations of new research in mathematics, physics, biology and computer science, almost daily.
- [Knowable Magazine](https://knowablemagazine.org/rss): Deeply reported stories from Annual Reviews that explain what scientists actually know, a couple of posts a week.
- [Hannah Ritchie](https://hannahritchie.substack.com/feed): Sustainability by Numbers, data-driven clarity on climate, energy and food from Our World in Data's lead researcher, every week or two.
- [Our World in Data](https://ourworldindata.org/atom.xml): Research and charts on the world's largest problems, from health to climate to poverty, a few posts a month.
- [Eric Topol](https://erictopol.substack.com/feed): Ground Truths, a leading cardiologist and researcher on the latest medical science, about weekly.
- [Carbon Brief](https://www.carbonbrief.org/feed/): Rigorous, accessible coverage of climate science, policy and energy, a few posts a week.
- [Dynomight](https://dynomight.net/feed.xml): Curious, careful essays that dig into scientific evidence on everyday questions, a couple of posts a month.
- [Asimov Press](https://www.asimov.press/feed): Long-form essays and fiction about biology, biotechnology and the future of science, a couple of posts a month.
- [The Last Word on Nothing](https://lastwordonnothing.com/feed/): A group of seasoned science journalists writing personal, surprising essays about science, several posts a week.
- [Aeon](https://aeon.co/feed.rss): Essays on science, philosophy and culture by researchers and thinkers, about one a day.
- [RealClimate](https://www.realclimate.org/index.php/feed/): Working climate scientists explain new research and correct misconceptions, a few posts a month.
- [Undark](https://undark.org/feed/): Independent journalism on science and its place in society, from public health to research ethics, several posts a week.
- [In the Pipeline](https://www.science.org/blogs/pipeline/feed): Chemist Derek Lowe's sharp, informed commentary on drug discovery and biomedical research, a few posts a week.
- [ESA Space Science](https://www.esa.int/rssfeed/Our_Activities/Space_Science): Missions, discoveries and explainers from the European Space Agency, a couple of posts a week.
- [NASA Image of the Day](https://www.nasa.gov/feeds/iotd-feed/): One striking image from NASA's missions and archives, with the story behind it, most days.
- [The Atlantic Science](https://www.theatlantic.com/feed/channel/science/): Thoughtful long-form science journalism, from biology to climate to space, a couple of posts a week.
- [Smithsonian Science & Nature](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/rss/science-nature/): Stories on nature, archaeology and the history of science, a couple of posts a week.
- [Mapping Ignorance](https://mappingignorance.org/feed/): Researchers explain recent findings across all fields of science, in plain language, most days.
