# Design

Curated by: Papercove  
Language: English  
Description: Interface, product and UX design: critique, process and inspiration worth slowing down for.

- [Brad Frost](https://bradfrost.com/feed/feed.xml): The author of Atomic Design on design systems, components and how design and engineering work together, about weekly.
- [Nielsen Norman Group](https://www.nngroup.com/feed/rss/): Research-backed UX articles on usability, interaction patterns and research methods, a few posts a week.
- [Stephanie Walter](https://stephaniewalter.design/feed/): A UX researcher and designer sharing practical resources on research, accessibility and inclusive design, a few posts a month.
- [Smashing Magazine — Design](https://www.smashingmagazine.com/category/design/index.xml): In-depth guides on UX, interface design and design process, about one a week.
- [Christopher Butler](https://www.chrbutler.com/feed.rss): Thoughtful essays on interface design, the web and designing with intent, every week or two.
- [Linear](https://linear.app/rss/now.xml): The Linear team on product craft, quality and how they design and build software, a couple of posts a month.
- [LukeW](https://www.lukew.com/ff/rss.asp): Luke Wroblewski's sharp notes on interface design, mobile and designing AI products, about weekly.
- [Figma Blog](https://www.figma.com/blog/feed/atom.xml): Design process, systems and stories from the Figma team and community, a few posts a week with bursts around launches.
- [Julie Zhuo](https://joulee.medium.com/feed): The author of The Making of a Manager on product design, leadership and building great teams, a few times a month.
- [UX Collective](https://uxdesign.cc/feed): The largest independent UX publication, with essays, case studies and a weekly curated digest. Several posts a day.
- [Tobias van Schneider](https://vanschneider.com/blog/rss/): Candid essays on the design craft, creativity and building products, about once a month.
- [GOV.UK Design Notes](https://designnotes.blog.gov.uk/feed/): How the UK government designs public digital services, with lessons on research, accessibility and plain language. Several posts a year.
- [Eric Bailey](https://ericwbailey.website/feed/feed.xml): Accessibility and inclusive design, explained with care and conviction, about once a month.
- [A List Apart](https://alistapart.com/main/feed/): The classic magazine for people who make websites, on design, content and accessibility. A few articles a year.
- [Boagworld](https://boagworld.com/feed/): Paul Boag's practical advice on UX strategy, conversion and making the case for design, a few posts a month.
- [HeyDesigner](https://heydesigner.com/rss.xml): Hand-picked links on UI design, design systems and design engineering, about one a day.
- [Christina Wodtke](https://eleganthack.com/feed/): Product design, team collaboration and goal-setting from the author of Radical Focus, a couple of posts a month.
- [Product Talk](https://www.producttalk.org/rss/): Teresa Torres on continuous discovery, user research and making better product decisions, about weekly.
- [Jorge Arango](https://jarango.com/feed): Information architecture, sensemaking and designing for AI, about weekly.
- [Ahmad Shadeed](https://ishadeed.com/feed.xml): Interactive deep dives into UI design and layout, with real interface examples, about once a month.
