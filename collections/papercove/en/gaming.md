# Gaming

Curated by: Papercove  
Language: English  
Description: Video game news, reviews and criticism, from big releases to small indie gems.

- [The Digital Antiquarian](https://www.filfre.net/feed/): Jimmy Maher's richly researched history of computer games and the people who made them, about one long essay a week.
- [Rock Paper Shotgun Reviews](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/feed/reviews): Witty, opinionated PC game reviews, from blockbusters to strange little indies, about one a week.
- [Crossplay](https://www.crossplay.news/feed): Patrick Klepek's newsletter on games, the people who play them and the industry around them, a couple of times a week.
- [Eurogamer Reviews](https://www.eurogamer.net/feed/reviews): Thoughtful reviews across PC and consoles, with room for smaller releases. A review or two a week.
- [Game File](https://www.gamefile.news/feed): Stephen Totilo's independent reporting on the games business, with interviews and scoops. Several posts a week.
- [Indie Games Plus](https://indiegamesplus.com/feed/): Spotlights on small, often free indie games you won't see elsewhere, a few posts a week.
- [Pushing Buttons](https://www.theguardian.com/games/series/pushing-buttons/rss): Keza MacDonald's weekly Guardian newsletter on what's worth playing and what's happening in games.
- [Thinky Games](https://thinkygames.com/feed/): The best place for puzzle and "thinky" games, mostly indie, with reviews, news and recommendations. A few posts a week.
- [GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/feed): Simon Carless on how games get found, with data on Steam trends and plenty of upcoming indie picks. A couple of posts a week.
- [Adventure Gamers](https://adventuregamers.com/feed): Reviews and news on adventure and narrative games, many of them indie, a couple of posts a week.
- [Jupiter Hadley](https://jupiterhadley.com/feed/): An indie game curator and jam judge sharing hidden gems from itch.io and beyond, almost daily.
- [Ars Technica Gaming](https://arstechnica.com/gaming/feed/): Kyle Orland and team on games, hardware and the business behind them, with a sharp technical eye. A few posts a week.
- [Radiator Blog](https://www.blog.radiator.debacle.us/feeds/posts/default): Indie developer Robert Yang on level design, game culture and making personal games, about once a month.
- [Guardian Games Reviews](https://www.theguardian.com/games+tone/reviews/rss): Concise, well-written reviews of big releases and indie surprises alike, a couple a week.
- [How to Market a Game](https://howtomarketagame.com/feed/): Chris Zukowski's practical advice for indie developers on Steam wishlists, launches and visibility, about weekly.
- [Warp Door](https://warpdoor.com/rss/): Short, enthusiastic picks of weird and wonderful indie games, mostly free and playable in minutes. About one or two a day.
- [The Game Business](https://www.thegamebusiness.com/feed): Christopher Dring's newsletter on how the games industry works, with sales data and interviews. A few posts a week.
- [Remap](https://remapradio.com/rss/): The worker-owned team behind the former Waypoint on games, culture and the industry, a couple of posts a week.
- [Destructoid Reviews](https://www.destructoid.com/reviews/feed/): Reviews of the big releases and plenty of mid-size games, about one or two a week.
- [Boiling Steam](https://boilingsteam.com/feed/rss-feed.xml): Gaming on Linux and the Steam Deck, with lots of indie coverage, interviews and data. A few posts a week.
